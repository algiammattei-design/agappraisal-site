Orange County, covered whole

Residential appraisals across Orlando and Orange County.

From Winter Park bungalows to new construction in Lake Nona, Orange County has some of the widest value swings in Central Florida, sometimes within a few blocks. That is where careful comparable selection earns its fee.

Where I work in Orange County

Orlando, Winter Park, Maitland, Apopka, Ocoee, Winter Garden, Windermere, Dr. Phillips, Lake Nona, Baldwin Park and the communities around them. If the address is in Orange County, it is in my area.

Why the neighborhood line matters

Two homes the same size can sit in different markets because of a school zone, a lake view, a historic district, or which side of a major road they are on. Knowing why two streets in Winter Garden are not comparable is the job. Every adjustment in the report is shown, so you, your attorney or your CPA can follow the reasoning.

Appraisals I do here most often

  • Date of death and estate appraisals
  • Divorce and equitable distribution
  • Property tax appeals for the Orange County Value Adjustment Board
  • PMI removal
  • Pre-listing and second-opinion appraisals, mortgage and refinance work, and investor as-is and as-repaired values

Tax appeals in Orange County: values are set by the Orange County Property Appraiser as of January 1, and petitions go to the Value Adjustment Board through the Orange County Comptroller.
